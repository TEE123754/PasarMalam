# Demo privacy and data map

Updated: 1 October 2026. Phase 2 introduces connected identity and protected records. All seeded identities, reviews, organisations and reports are fictional. Real personal-data processing/pilot use requires a documented Malaysia PDPA review, provider and cross-border assessment, operational rights handling and approved retention decisions. This document is an engineering plan, not a legal compliance claim.

Official reference: [Malaysia Personal Data Protection Commissioner](https://www.pdp.gov.my/). Review the applicable current Act, amendments and guidance with the pilot owner before collecting real records.

## Rights and real-pilot readiness

Phase 9 adds explicit simulated consent before an internal referral and preserves the Phase 7 independent-report/reply gates. Only moderators can approve a case copy; authority membership scopes access to that copy. No original evidence is copied or shared. Case/log text still requires human redaction review because phone/email masks do not guarantee de-identification. Council complaint/waste aggregates suppress fewer than five independent stalls; collection receipts/weights are synthetic and scoped. No real agency is contacted. Operational retention/deletion and real consent remain part of later pilot review.

Phase 7: raw complaints/evidence are restricted to the reporter and authorised moderator. Organisers receive only market-scoped generic early notices or separately approved redacted summaries; hawkers receive approved summaries only. Reply/flag publication requires recorded moderation; internal independence groups/reasons are not public. Drafts clear on persona/session changes, and review editors are separate from confidential report editors. Complaint videos are optional MP4 (10 seconds/5 MB), validated/transcoded with FFmpeg, stripped of audio/metadata and removed from temporary disk after processing. If the runtime is absent, video is rejected. Evidence signed links last 60 seconds; permitted viewers can still save copies. Placeholder fixture evidence contains descriptions only.

Closed-complaint/evidence 90-day retention is a proposed pilot decision, not an enabled purge. Approve lawful exceptions/appeals, operational access review and an auditable deletion procedure before real collection. Local demo reset never deletes connected Auth/production accounts. Phone/email masking helps human redaction but cannot guarantee all identifiers are removed. Referral drafts contain approved summaries and fictional routing context only; they do not leave the app.

Phase 6 evaluation tasks store anonymous pair/clip IDs, consent acknowledgement, device/network/order and durations in separate demo/real `sessionStorage` keys. These records remain in that tab until it closes and contain no raw audio or participant names. Export is explicit. Private consented corpus audio is handled separately; supplied-file audio stays in memory and cannot qualify as a timed microphone task. Synthetic smoke recordings in ignored `.local-voice/` are developer fixtures, not retained user voice notes. The local model key and weights remain ignored; extraction is authenticated loopback only.

Demo testers contact the organiser through the pilot's agreed channel; no real contact directory is invented here. An operator verifies the account, exports only its permitted records, corrects requested profile fields, and handles deletion through a reviewed scoped procedure. The current guarded reset refuses existing batches/reports and preserves Auth accounts and audit history. It is not a user-erasure endpoint.

Before a real pilot, approve notices in BM/English, controller/processor responsibilities, provider location/terms, cross-border transfers, security/incident procedures, access/correction/withdrawal/deletion requests, and actual retention jobs. Optional location/voice consent must not block basic discovery or manual listings. No documents, complaints or voice samples from real people should be placed into fixtures or public screenshots.

## Phase 10 data

Advanced fixtures keep four invented recipient IDs/coarse cells/preferences and fake inboxes under `pasarmalam:demo:advanced-v1`; no real SMS/email contacts exist. Research/AI/sensor state cannot write batch deadlines or publish listings. Synthetic analytics history is distinct from commerce/collection ledgers and measured speech evidence. Demo reset deletes only that local fixture data.

Connected test push stores one private subscription per account (endpoint, encryption keys and consent time); only authenticated enrol/revoke RPCs mutate it. Keys are not exposed by public/admin browser table access or history projections. Server-only VAPID private key authenticates the test transport. Coarse nearby consent expires after two hours; default radius is 1.5 km and optional Malaysian quiet hours. Browser permission is an explicit separate user gesture. Provider acceptance is not device/read receipt; acknowledgement remains uncollected/null. Revocation deletes credentials and cancels later queue work; a previously accepted notification cannot be recalled and an in-flight send can hold the recipient lock for the bounded transport timeout. Private attempt logs contain event/outcome/time, not credentials or message content. Operator retention for attempts/outbox and ordinary credential rotation remains a pre-pilot decision. Test push sends to the browser's allowlisted Google/Mozilla/Apple technical push service only; no SMS/email or business authority service is contacted.

## Phase 11 presentation data
The catalog, pilot rows, asset descriptions and diagram clips are invented public-safe static content. Generic historical groups are reference examples, separate from actionable portal ledgers and actual measured evaluation. Complete reset removes only explicitly named local demo/fixture state, preserves real connected accounts and sends nothing. Draft posters use a same-device localhost QR until approved deployment. User images, real audio consent and full connected membership provisioning remain pending.
