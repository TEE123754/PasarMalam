## English

Hi! I'm Ruben, a UTM AI student. I'm running a short evaluation for my AI-EXPO project — a pasar malam app that understands spoken instructions (BM/English/mixed) for updating stall menus and batches. Could you spare 15–20 minutes? Just a few sentences to record + trying the app briefly. Your info stays anonymous (ID only, no names). Keen to help?

## Who to ask

- Need **5 adults minimum**, mixing hawkers and customers (the checklist doesn't fix a ratio — e.g. 2–3 hawkers you know + 2–3 customers/friends/family is fine).
- Prioritise people comfortable switching between BM/English/Manglish naturally — that's the hardest case for the model.
- Family, coursemates, and nearby hawkers you already have rapport with are the fastest path — this doesn't need to be a public stranger survey.
