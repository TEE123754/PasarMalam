# PasarMalam evaluation — interview template

Participant ID: S0_ 　 Role: ☐ Hawker ☐ Customer 　 Date: __________ 　 Consent form filed: ☐ Yes

Run this *before* the participant tries the app, so their answers reflect current real-world behaviour, not the app's influence.

## Current workflow (ask all participants)

1. How do you currently update your menu or let customers know what's available tonight? *(Hawkers)* / How do you currently find out what stalls/dishes are open tonight? *(Customers)*
2. How do you currently track batches of food — e.g. "this batch was cooked at 6pm, make sure it's sold/eaten by a certain time"? *(Hawkers)* / How do you currently judge whether food at a stall is fresh? *(Customers)*
3. What do you currently do with unsold or leftover food at the end of the night?
4. How do you currently discover new stalls or markets, or decide where to go?
5. What's the most annoying or time-consuming part of this process today?

## After they try the app (hands-on, see trial-task-template)

6. What was confusing or didn't work the way you expected?
7. Were there any tasks you failed or gave up on? Which ones, and why?
8. What would you change about how the app asks for information (voice or typing)?
9. Would you actually use something like this? Why or why not?
10. Anything else you'd want it to do that it doesn't?
