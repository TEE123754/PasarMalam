# PasarMalam - Your Step-by-Step Action Guide

Prepared: **9 October 2026, Malaysia time**.

This guide covers your actions for all **27 open checklist items**, additional device/privacy/image checks, and later commercial activation. The app currently has **124 items checked in its local scope**, including labelled simulations. Your actions below are not marked complete merely because a form, template or software test exists.

Use the checkboxes to track your work. Keep participant recordings, consent, student records, agreements and billing evidence privately; share anonymous findings in the competition materials. Do not put passwords or API keys in this file.

## Step 1 - Confirm your team and academic supervisor

**Checklist item: C15-11.**

- [ ] Confirm that every student participant is currently registered with UTM's Faculty of Artificial Intelligence.
- [ ] Confirm your competition level: undergraduate or postgraduate.
- [ ] For UG, confirm 2-3 student members. For PG, confirm an individual entry or up to three students. Do not combine UG and PG in one team.
- [ ] Obtain at least one academic supervisor's agreement to supervise the entry.
- [ ] Identify the student who will present or narrate the English video.
- [ ] Record verified team/programme/supervisor details in the private CP9 entry template.

**Evidence to retain:** verified entry details and supervisor confirmation. Keep personal student documents private.

**Next technical work:** the evidence pack can be updated with your verified details; software cannot establish eligibility.

## Step 2 - Confirm SantanSafe history and review MVP maturity

**Checklist items: C15-09 and C15-10.**

- [ ] Confirm whether SantanSafe or PasarMalam had an earlier developed, presented, published or competition-entered version.
- [ ] If yes, record its date, scope and evidence, then identify implemented improvements in the current version.
- [ ] If no previous version exists, confirm that fact explicitly; do not leave the declaration inferred or blank.
- [ ] Review the current app and test evidence with your supervisor using the CP9 evidence pack.
- [ ] Document why the evidence supports the required TRL 4 or above, including limitations and what has actually been validated.
- [ ] Resolve any uncertainty about the organiser's maturity requirements before submission; do not assign a TRL from test counts alone.

**Evidence to retain:** prior-version declaration, before/after capability comparison where applicable, and a reviewed maturity assessment.

**Use:** [CP9 evidence and declaration pack](CP9_EVIDENCE_PACK.md).

## Step 3 - Review privacy and participant consent before collection

**Additional acceptance: A05; also affects registration and human testing.**

- [ ] Identify the accountable app/research operator and a contact for questions, withdrawal and deletion requests.
- [ ] Review what becomes public, what stays private, who can access it, and how long it is retained.
- [ ] Review the optional business declarations and complaint identity protections.
- [ ] Review the external AI provider's processing/retention arrangements; app-side deletion is not proof of provider deletion.
- [ ] Prepare separate consent for recording, interviews/tasks, and any redacted transcript sent to the external model.
- [ ] Agree a withdrawal/deletion process with participants and retain consent receipts privately.
- [ ] Keep generated images, scripted examples and simulated sales distinct from real participant evidence.

**Evidence to retain:** dated review decisions, notice version, consent wording and private receipts. This is a review task, not an automatic declaration of legal compliance.

**Use:** [Privacy notice and review draft](CP1_PRIVACY_REVIEW.md) and [recording consent guide](VOICE_RECORDING_GUIDE.md).

## Step 4 - Decide registration policy and test an actual account

**Checklist item: C01-03.**

- [ ] Choose invitation-only registration for the controlled pilot, or request public registration for the intended release.
- [ ] Provide one consenting test participant and an email address they control. Do not post passwords in chat.
- [ ] Arrange the approved test configuration before starting; public signup is currently disabled.
- [ ] Test invitation/registration, email verification, login, profile editing, sign-out and subsequent login.
- [ ] Check that a new customer cannot obtain hawker permissions simply by switching modes.
- [ ] Record actual outcomes and errors; review the results against the agreed policy.

**Evidence to retain:** a dated account journey with private details removed.

**Next technical work:** configuration and any failed-journey repairs can follow your policy decision. A coded registration screen alone does not close this item.

## Step 5 - Arrange a qualified food-safety review

**Checklist items: C07-01 and C07-13.**

- [ ] Ask your supervisor to help identify a qualified food-safety reviewer.
- [ ] Give the reviewer the policy package and explain the supported ambient, hot-held, chilled and unknown conditions.
- [ ] Ask them to review holding-time rules, required measurements, uncertainty, storage changes and cumulative exposure.
- [ ] Record the approved scope, references, rule version, reviewer, date, rejected assumptions and unresolved cases.
- [ ] Provide the review decisions so the cumulative-exposure logic and matching test cases can be completed.
- [ ] Review the implemented results after the approved changes are made.

**Evidence to retain:** dated, specific review findings and the subsequent implementation/test record.

**Use:** [Food-policy review package](CP4_POLICY_REVIEW.md).

**If review is unavailable before submission:** keep the current policy labelled experimental and demonstrate fictional batches. Do not claim certification, KKM approval or proven food safety. A government partnership is a separate future action.

## Step 6 - Recruit consenting speakers, hawkers and customers

**Checklist items: C14-01 and C14-02; supports the remaining human AI items.**

- [ ] Recruit at least five consenting adult speakers for the prepared recording pack; include BM, English and mixed-language speakers.
- [ ] Include actual hawkers and customers in interviews and usability tasks; record the achieved numbers in each role.
- [ ] Use anonymous participant IDs such as S01-S05 rather than names in results.
- [ ] Ask about current listing effort, batch tracking, leftovers, promotions, discovery, trust and willingness to reuse the app.
- [ ] Record findings, difficulties and requested changes without inventing testimonials.
- [ ] Run a small documented trial using fictional food/batch scenarios until real-food and privacy acceptance is ready.

**Evidence to retain:** private consent receipts and anonymised interview/trial records, including participant counts and limitations.

**Use:** [Measurement guide](CP7_MEASUREMENT_GUIDE.md), [interview template](cp7-interviews.csv) and [trial template](cp7-trial.csv).

## Step 7 - Collect and freeze the voice test set

**Checklist items: C14-03 and C05-05.**

- [ ] Use the prepared 24-case pack: 10 BM, 10 Manglish and four English cases.
- [ ] Record one separate audio file per case, using the fixed fictional interpretation clock specified in the guide.
- [ ] Include the planned local-expression, bundle-price, batch-reference, ambiguity and noise cases.
- [ ] For controlled background noise, avoid recording non-consenting bystanders and describe how the noise was produced.
- [ ] Record speaker ID, device, language, condition and optional self-described accent.
- [ ] Listen to each recording and annotate what was actually said, including prices, quantities, dates/times and batch references.
- [ ] Have a competent bilingual reviewer check the expected answers before model inference.
- [ ] Keep development/practice examples separate, then freeze the held-out recordings and annotations before testing.

**Evidence to retain:** recordings, consent, verified annotations and frozen manifest in the private CP7 folder.

**Next technical work:** I can help check manifest integrity and prepare the scoring run. Do not change annotations to match a model's answer or tune on the held-out examples while retaining a held-out claim.

## Step 8 - Run real AI tests and typing-versus-voice tasks

**Checklist items: C14-04, C14-05 and C14-08.**

- [ ] Run each frozen recording through actual transcription and extraction; retain the uncorrected draft and every failure.
- [ ] Confirm that ambiguous time/price/batch cases request clarification and do not silently publish guessed data.
- [ ] Aim for 12 completed paired tasks using the same participant, facts, device and network for typing and live voice.
- [ ] Alternate which mode runs first. Time from task presentation through successful confirmation, including corrections.
- [ ] Separate cold model downloads from warm runs; file-based inference is not a live voice speed measurement.
- [ ] Record request response times, failed/retried attempts, actual usage and billing evidence where available.
- [ ] Leave unknown costs unknown; do not label a service free without evidence.

**Evidence to retain:** raw inference outputs, paired timings, corrections, request logs and private billing references. The numerical targets are project evaluation targets, not competition eligibility rules.

**Next technical work:** I can score field accuracy, compare timings and calculate supported latency/failure/cost totals. You must supply actual observations; corrected drafts cannot be scored as raw AI success.

## Step 9 - Check the app on a physical phone

**Additional acceptance: A01, A02 and A03.**

- [ ] Provide the phone model, OS and browser version, and identify its consenting tester.
- [ ] Arrange a supported secure connection for microphone/push testing. Phone localhost points to the phone, not your computer.
- [ ] Test microphone allow/deny, model download, transcription and manual fallback.
- [ ] Test BM/English labels, readable controls, keyboard, touch navigation and hawker/customer journeys.
- [ ] Test slow network, reconnect, interrupted recording and clear failure feedback.
- [ ] Test PWA installation, app update and restart on the intended browser.
- [ ] After cache-ready acknowledgement, disconnect the browser network, reload and visit an unvisited route; record any failure and unavailable online features.
- [ ] Opt in to a test notification and observe the actual displayed notification, then tap it and check the destination.
- [ ] Record independent batch timers and expired-offer behaviour after reconnect/resume.

**Evidence to retain:** actual device matrix, dated observations and relevant screenshots/video. A desktop mobile-sized viewport or successful outbox row does not complete these checks.

**Next technical work:** local secure access and repairs can be prepared from your device details. Physical-device interaction and permission decisions belong to the tester.

## Step 11 - Record the participating-student English video

**Checklist item: C15-08.**

## Step 14 - Validate forecasting with real history when available

**Checklist items: C12-01, C12-04, C12-05 and C14-11.**

- [ ] Obtain a stall's permission to use its historical sales/stock records.
- [ ] Gather at least 30 usable sessions for the same stall and weekday; existing consented history may be necessary.
- [ ] Record preparation date, prepared quantity, normal sales, completed clearance, closing leftovers and reconciliation status.
- [ ] Preserve missing/disrupted/stockout sessions and exclusion reasons; do not fabricate records or convert unknown counts to zero.
- [ ] Freeze the history/method before inspecting held-out results.
- [ ] Run and review actual forecast errors, baseline comparison, uncertainty coverage and likely-unsold performance.
- [ ] Retain all candidate results, including poor performance, and use a new untouched period after tuning.

**Evidence to retain:** consented history, source references and reviewed real benchmark.

**Use:** [Forecast collection guide](CP8_FORECAST_GUIDE.md).

A four-week weekly-market pilot cannot supply 30 same-weekday sessions. Until sufficient data exists, keep forecasting experimental/manual and exclude real accuracy claims from the entry.
