# PasarMalam pilot plan — one market, 10 stalls, four weeks

This is a proposed organiser-approved pilot, not a trial already conducted. The app's studio shows a synthetic example at fictional Seri Pelita. Choose one actual weekly market and ten consenting stalls only after handling-policy, moderation, privacy/retention, dietary-claim and provider eligibility review.

| Week | Activity | Evidence to collect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Organiser approval, baseline observation, hawker onboarding, voice/chips training | Scheduled/open/participating stall-nights, surplus before intervention, batch times, explicitly weighed discard, consented task timings |
| 2 | Pay-at-stall clearance trial inside advisory windows | Gross eligible collected revenue, refunds, hypothetical/actual agreed commission, incremental packaging costs, no-shows, weighed food actually transferred for human consumption |
| 3 | Continue trial; address opt-out/report/handling problems | Same definitions as week 2; record weather, stockouts, unchanged original deadlines and daily waste routes |
| 4 | Review with hawkers/organiser; decide whether another pilot is warranted | Compare baseline versus trial participation and distributions; identify missing weights, disputes and uncertainty; do not claim causality from four weeks |

A weekly market has four scheduled nights during a four-week pilot. Ten stalls × four nights = at most 40 stall-nights, not 280. Track cancellations/closures and actual participation separately. The synthetic example has 39 participating stall-nights because one stall is absent in week 2; that absence contributes neither value nor weight. Week 1 is a baseline row with no clearance recovery, not a fabricated sale.

Net recovered sen = completed eligible clearance gross − refunds − agreed commission − incremental fulfilment/packaging cost. Divide by participating stall-nights in the reported period; show the denominator and all components. Use integer sen and retain refund/reversal history. Bookings, pretend wallet attempts, cancelled orders and no-shows do not count as recovery. A zero denominator produces “No participation”, not RM0 performance. The studio's 3% commission is a synthetic hypothesis, not a fee charged.

Human-consumption diversion kg = deduplicated, completed eligible surplus transfers with explicitly weighed net food mass, divided by 1000. Count each batch-transfer receipt once; exclude rejected, missed, returned, expired or non-consumption transfers. Keep estimated weight separate and report weighing coverage. Compost and BSF have separate kg totals and never enter human-consumption diversion. Do not add estimated and weighed values. Shopper intention/reservations are not evidence of food consumed.

Report medians/spread as well as totals where actual data permits. Keep temperature/humidity/handling uncertainty and stockouts as context; the pilot cannot establish scientific safety thresholds or validate a demand model without further review. Public hygiene flags still require verified independent reports, moderation and the hawker reply opportunity; organiser dashboards suppress small groups. A real commission pilot requires review of free-service commercial eligibility before collecting money through the business.

Cold start: enrol hawkers first with the organiser, then invite shoppers using reviewed QR posters/community copy. The generated WhatsApp/Facebook/TikTok drafts are never posted automatically. Use an approved public preview origin; localhost QR is a same-device development example. Basic functionality stays free and usable through chips/manual entry. Consent for voice, coarse location and notifications is optional and separate. Add languages after measured BM/English/Manglish usability evidence.
