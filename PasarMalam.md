\# Product Requirement Document (PRD) \*\*Product Name:\*\* PasarMalam (Community Platform) \*\*Document Version:\*\* 1.0 \*\*Target Platform:\*\* Mobile App (iOS / Android / PWA) \*\*Primary Focus:\*\* Food Waste Mitigation (SDG 12, SDG 2), Street Food Safety (SDG 3), and Hyper-Local Micro-Merchant Commerce (SDG 8, SDG 11) --- ## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement ### 1.1 The Problem Malaysia's morning markets (\*pasar pagi\* / \*pasar tani\*) and night markets (\*pasar malam\*) are vital socio-economic hubs, yet they face two opposing structural crises: 1. \*\*Massive Perishable Food Waste:\*\* Cooked street food and fresh delicacies generated by hundreds of stalls are routinely dumped into landfills between 9:30 PM and 11:00 PM if unsold. Hawkers lack a real-time, low-friction channel to clear inventory. 2. \*\*Biological Food Safety Risks:\*\* In tropical ambient heat ($30\degree\text{C} - 34\degree\text{C}$), cooked dishes enter the Ministry of Health (KKM) Temperature Danger Zone ($5\degree\text{C} - 60\degree\text{C}$). High-risk ingredients—specifically coconut milk (\*santan\*), gravies, poultry, and cooked starch—breed \*Bacillus cereus\* and \*Staphylococcus aureus\* toxins within 2 to 4 hours. Hawkers do not track holding times across batches, while consumers have no transparency regarding when food was cooked. 3. \*\*High Digital Friction for Hawkers:\*\* Street food operators cannot navigate complex e-commerce forms while serving customers with wet or oily hands. ### 1.2 The Solution \*\*PasarMalam\*\* is an ambient-aware community platform connecting morning and night market hawkers with nearby consumers. It eliminates manual typing by using \*\*multilingual voice and photo intake\*\* (Bahasa Melayu, English, Manglish), models \*\*bio-thermal food safety decay\*\* across cooking batches, alerts hawkers to launch 1-tap clearance promotions before food degrades, and provides consumers with live stall maps, hygiene ratings, and anonymous whistleblowing channels. --- ## 2. Functional Specifications & Feature Modules ### Module 1: Market & Stall Discovery (Consumer & Hawker Mode) \* \*\*Dual-Market Scheduling Engine:\*\* \* Supports both \*\*Pasar Pagi / Pasar Tani\*\* (06:00 – 11:30) and \*\*Pasar Malam\*\* (16:30 – 22:30). \* Auto-activates market zones on the map based on municipal operating schedules (e.g., SS2 Night Market on Mondays, Seksyen 7 Shah Alam on Wednesdays). \* \*\*Interactive Geospatial Pinpointing:\*\* \* Map pins show active pasar malam grounds with real-time operational status (Opening Soon, Active, Closing Down). \* Tapping a market expands a micro-directory of all active stalls clustered inside that lot. \* \*\*Faceted Search & Filter Engine:\*\* \* \*\*Proximity:\*\* Nearest to user ($< 500\text{m}$, $< 2\text{km}$, $< 5\text{km}$). \* \*\*Dietary Assurance:\*\* Halal-Certified, Muslim-Owned, Pork-Free / Alcohol-Free, Vegetarian / Buddhist Vegan. \* \*\*Food Categories:\*\* Cooked Rice & Noodles, Skewers & BBQ (\*Lok-Lok\*, Satay), Pastries & \*Kuih\*, Beverages, Fresh Produce, Deep Fried (\*Goreng-Goreng\*). \* \*\*Sorting Vectors:\*\* Top Rated, Most Popular (crowd check-ins), and Active Flash Discounts. --- ### Module 2: Zero-Typing Hawker Stall & Batch Management \* \*\*Multimodal Voice & Photo Listing:\*\* \* Hawkers upload a photo of their stall banner and food trays. \* Hawkers record a 5-to-10 second voice note in conversational Bahasa Melayu, English, or Manglish: > \*"Hello, gerai Nasi Lemak Royale dah buka tapak nombor 14. Ada rendang ayam dan sambal kerang, siap masak pukul 5 petang. Satu bungkus RM6."\* \* The backend structures this audio/photo into: \* \`stall\_name\`: "Nasi Lemak Royale" \* \`lot\_number\`: "14" \* \`menu\_items\`: \`\[{"item": "Rendang Ayam", "price": 6.00}, {"item": "Sambal Kerang", "price": 6.00}\]\` \* \`prep\_timestamp\`: 17:00:00 \* \`ingredient\_profile\`: \`\[SANTAN, POULTRY, SHELLFISH\]\` \* \*\*Batch Tracking Architecture:\*\* \* When a hawker fries or cooks a new pot of the same dish, they click a single button: \*\*"New Batch Prepared"\*\* (or say: \*"Mee goreng kuali kedua siap masak"\*). \* System registers \`Batch #2\`, timestamps the event, resets the biological freshness countdown, and leaves any unsold items from \`Batch #1\` under the older countdown window. --- ### Module 3: Bio-Thermal Food Safety Calculator & Automated Alerts \* \*\*Regulatory Baseline:\*\* Complies with Ministry of Health (KKM) Food Hygiene Regulations: cooked food must not remain in ambient room conditions exceeding the Danger Zone thresholds. \* \*\*Dynamic Shelf-Life Decay Algorithm:\*\* The safe consumption window ($T\_{\text{safe}}$ in minutes) is dynamically derived using: $$T\_{\text{safe}} = \frac{\text{BaseHoldingTime}}{1 + \alpha (T\_{\text{ambient}} - 25\degree\text{C})} \times \omega\_{\text{ingredient}}$$| Parameter | Value / Range | Definition | | :--- | :--- | :--- | | \*\*$\\text{BaseHoldingTime}$\*\* | 240 mins (4 hours) | Maximum allowable KKM holding period at$25\\degree\\text{C}$room temp. | | \*\*$T\_{\\text{ambient}}$\*\* | Sourced via Open-Meteo API | Real-time street-level temperature at the market's GPS coordinate. | | \*\*$\\alpha$\*\* | 0.05 | Thermal acceleration coefficient. | | \*\*$\\omega\_{\\text{ingredient}}$\*\* | \`0.50\` (Critical Risk) | High-spoilage: Coconut milk (\*santan\*), chicken, eggs, seafood gravies. | | | \`0.75\` (Moderate Risk) | Fried carbs: Fried rice (\*nasi goreng\*), fried noodles (\*char kway teow\*). | | | \`1.50\` (Low Risk) | Dry snacks: Curry puffs (\*karipap\*), apam balik, dry fried pastries. | \* \*\*Real-World Calculation Example:\*\* \* Item: \*Nasi Lemak Ayam Santan\* ($\\omega = 0.50$). Nasi Lemak RM3.00 di Gerai No. 14 (Tinggal 20 minit)."\* --- ### Module 5: Community Trust, Reviews & Anonymous Whistleblowing \* \*\*Consumer Reviews & Freshness Verification:\*\* \* Star ratings (1 to 5) and photo uploads. \* Verified Freshness Badge: Consumers verify if the food was served hot and within the stated batch window. \* \*\*Anonymous Food Safety Whistleblower Channel:\*\* \* Consumers can submit confidential hygiene reports directly through the app. \* \*\*Complaint Categories:\*\* Sours/Spoiled Food, Pest Sightings (rats, flies, cockroaches), Cross-Contamination (raw meat mixed with cooked food), Unhygienic Handling (no hairnet, handling food with bare unwashed hands). \* Users can upload photo/video proof with geo-stamp verification. \* Protects consumer privacy while accumulating a vendor risk index score. --- ## 5.
